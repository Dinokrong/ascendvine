Market Knowledge & Current Events

IB interviews regularly test your awareness of current market conditions, key indices, and your ability to discuss a recent deal. Staying current signals genuine interest in finance.

Key Numbers to Know

You should always know current approximate figures for these items going into any interview. Update these regularly.

  • S&P 500 level — approximate current level and YTD return
  • 10-Year Treasury yield — critical for WACC / risk-free rate
  • Fed Funds Rate — current rate and direction of policy
  • US GDP growth rate — recent quarter and annual estimate
  • Unemployment rate
  • Inflation (CPI YoY)
  • Dollar index (DXY) — directional move matters
  • Crude oil price (WTI / Brent)
  • Investment grade / high yield credit spreads

How to Discuss a Recent Deal

You will almost certainly be asked: "Tell me about a recent deal that interests you." Framework for a strong answer:

  1. Parties involved: Who is the acquirer and who is the target?
  2. Deal structure: How much? Cash, stock, or mix? Any debt financing?
  3. Strategic rationale: Why did the acquirer buy this target? Cost synergies, revenue synergies, geographic expansion, technology acquisition?
  4. Valuation: What multiple did they pay? Is that high or low vs. precedents?
  5. Your view: Was this a good deal? Who benefits more?
Pick a deal in the sector the group covers. Showing sector awareness is far more impressive than picking a famous but unrelated deal.

Macro Themes to Be Able to Discuss

  • Current Fed policy direction (hiking / cutting / pausing) and its effect on deal activity and valuations
  • M&A environment: is activity up or down? What's driving it?
  • IPO / capital markets environment: is it open or closed?
  • Credit markets: are spreads tight or wide? Impact on LBO financing
  • AI and technology disruption: how is it affecting specific industries?

Staying Current — Daily Habits

  • Read the WSJ, FT, or Bloomberg daily (15 minutes minimum)
  • Track the M&A section of Reuters / Bloomberg for recent deals
  • Follow sector-specific news for the group you're interviewing with
  • Listen to earnings calls of large companies in the sector
  • Review weekly equity research from bulge brackets
