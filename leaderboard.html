How team scores are kept fair

Teams differ in size and in how many freshmen and sophomores they have, so a team's score isn't a total:

  1. Compared with your own class year. On each quiz, your score is compared with the average for your graduation year. 100 = exactly average for your year; 115 = 15% above it.
  2. Missed quizzes count as 0 once grading has started for that quiz in your group. A quiz still waiting for a grade is left out until it's graded.
  3. Team score = the average of its members, so a 4-person team and a 6-person team compete evenly.
